Tower Semiconductor Reports Third Quarter Record Results, Providing Record Fourth Quarter Guidance Resulting in 2026 $2 Billion Annual Revenue

November 2, 2026 at 7:00 AM EST
PDF Version
Third quarter revenue of $526 million, representing 33% year-over-year growth; Guiding fourth quarter revenue of $600 million, 36% year-over-year growth

MIGDAL HAEMEK, Israel, Nov. 02, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Tower Semiconductor (NASDAQ/TASE: TSEM) reports today its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter of 2026 Results Overview
Record revenue for the third quarter of 2026 of $526 million, representing revenue growth of 33% year over year, as compared to $396 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Record gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 of $179 million, 92% higher than $93 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Operating profit for the third quarter of 2026 was a record of $127 million (excluding non-recurring items), a multiple of 2.5X as compared to the $51 million operating profit for the third quarter of 2025.

Net profit for the third quarter of 2026 was a record of $120 million (excluding non-recurring items), representing 23% net margin and reflecting $1.06 basic and $1.05 diluted earnings per share. This net profit is a multiple of 2.2X of the $54 million net profit in the third quarter of 2025.

Cash from operating activities in the third quarter of 2026 was $185 million and investments in property and equipment, net, were $191 million. In the third quarter of 2025, cash flow generated from operating activities was $139 million and investments in property and equipment, net, were $103 million.

Business Outlook
The Company provides revenue guidance of $600 million for the fourth quarter of 2026, with an upward or downward range of 5%. Mid-range revenue guidance represents an increase of 36% year-over-year and 14% quarter-over-quarter.

Russell Ellwanger, Chief Executive Officer of Tower Semiconductor, said: “We are entering the strongest growth phase in our history, according to fourth quarter guidance, closing the year at $2 billion annual revenue and a $2.4 billion annualized revenue run rate. We target to begin 2027 avoiding first quarter’s typical industry seasonality, with clear visibility to incremental sequential revenues with accompanying margins’ growth.”

Ellwanger further added: “With a background of growth and strong increasing demand in each of our core businesses, infrastructure (SiPho and SiGe) demand specifically is driving exceptional growth in our high-speed connectivity business. We target to achieve an annualized $1.1 billion run rate in the fourth quarter of 2026 of SiPho revenue alone, namely 5X of the 2025 SiPho annual revenue. Our on-going capacity investments, including the announced Track-1 Japan 300mm buildout, are progressing ahead of schedule, supported by customer contracts which are being extended through 2029, providing great assurance to achieve our 2028 revenue and margins model, and a well-defined path for accretive margins thereafter. The combination of Tower’s technology leadership, expanding capacity and deep alignment with our customers, positions Tower to deliver significant long-term growth and shareholder value.”

About Tower Semiconductor
Tower Semiconductor Ltd. (NASDAQ/TASE: TSEM) is a leading foundry of high-value analog semiconductor solutions, serving growing markets including industrial, automotive, mobile, infrastructure, medical, and aerospace & defense. The company offers a broad portfolio of advanced and innovative analog technologies, including silicon photonics (SiPho), SiGe, RF CMOS, CMOS image sensors, power management, and mixed-signal, supported by world-class design enablement and process transfer services. Tower operates a global manufacturing network with 200mm and 300mm fabs in Israel, the U.S., Japan, and Italy, providing customers with scalable capacity and multi-fab sourcing. Learn more at www.towersemi.com.
