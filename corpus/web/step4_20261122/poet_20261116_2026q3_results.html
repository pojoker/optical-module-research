POET Technologies Reports Record Q3 2026 Revenue of $1.03M, Up 244%

Seventh consecutive quarter of sequential growth; loss per share improves to $(0.04) from $(0.07); $774.3 million in cash and short-term investments

November 16, 2026 07:00 ET  | Source: POET Technologies Inc. POET Technologies Inc.

TORONTO, Nov. 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POET Technologies Inc. (NASDAQ: POET) ("POET" or the "Company"), a leading designer and developer of photonic integrated circuits, light sources and optical engines for data center and AI networks, today reported record revenue of $1,026,087 for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 80% from the second quarter of 2026 and 244% from the third quarter of 2025. It was the Company's seventh consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth. Net loss was $7.6 million, or $(0.04) per share, improved from $11.3 million, or $(0.07) per share, in the second quarter of 2026 and from $9.4 million, or $(0.11) per share, in the third quarter of 2025. Cash and short-term investments were $774.3 million at quarter-end, and cash used in operations during the quarter was $12.7 million. The Company's unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements and Management's Discussion and Analysis for the quarter have been filed on SEDAR+ and as exhibits to a Form 6-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission via EDGAR. All figures are in U.S. dollars.

Key Metrics

  • Revenue: $1,026,087, up 80% from the second quarter of 2026 and 244% from the third quarter of 2025
  • Sequential revenue growth: seven consecutive quarters
  • Net loss per share: $(0.04), improved from $(0.07) in the second quarter of 2026
  • Cash and short-term investments: $774.3 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $796.3 million at June 30, 2026
  • Cash used in operations: $12.7 million in the third quarter
  • Wafer capacity: approximately tenfold increase secured at the Company's foundry partner for 2027
  • Engine assembly capacity: projected approximately tenfold increase by the end of 2027 aligned with demand
  • Active customers: 9, across 16 projects; 7 expected to enter production with POET engines in 2027
  • Team: 164 employees at September 30, 2026

Management Commentary

"Our first quarter above a million dollars in revenue, and seven straight quarters of growth, come from programs we spent years building and are now moving into production," said Dr. Suresh Venkatesan, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. "In the quarter we signed a development agreement with a Tier 1 laser company for an external light source engine and received a $2.4 million purchase order from an existing customer. We are scaling capacity approximately tenfold in both wafers and engine assembly to serve the seven of our nine active customers we expect to move into production in 2027, and we have grown the team to 164 people to run it."

"We expect to ship our current backlog of optical engines, including the 30,000 engines we had targeted for 2026, by the second quarter of 2027, as the capacity we have already funded comes online," Dr. Venkatesan added. "Like much of the industry, we have faced supply chain tightening, wafer and component shortages, and are working through a yield ramp of a new production technology this year. We have secured roughly ten times our existing wafer capacity and are installing the equipment now. Until it is in place, we are sequencing wafer starts between production orders and new customer programs, so we ship to our customers' schedules and keep our new products on track. Our shipments in the current quarter will exceed those of the first nine months of the year combined."

"We have the balance sheet to build at scale and the customer programs to build for," Dr. Venkatesan added. "With Giovanni Pacelli joining as Chief Financial Officer, it’s time to report on that progress on a regular basis, beginning with our first quarterly earnings call on March 1, 2027."

"POET ended the quarter with $774.3 million in cash and short-term investments," said Giovanni Pacelli, Chief Financial Officer. "The reported net loss narrowed in part because of a $6.4 million non-cash gain on the revaluation of warrants. Cash used in operations was $12.7 million, essentially unchanged from the second quarter. Beginning with our fourth-quarter and full-year results on March 1, 2027, POET will report as a U.S. domestic issuer, with financial statements prepared under U.S. GAAP."

Capacity and Production Update

  • Wafers. For much of 2026 the Company ran at approximately 120 wafers per month at its foundry partner in Malaysia, up from approximately 50 wafers per month in 2025. It has now committed tools for approximately 1,250 wafers per month, rising to approximately 1,500 with planned productivity improvements, a roughly tenfold increase.
  • Engine assembly. The Company is expanding engine assembly in two phases with its contract manufacturers in Penang. The first phase is in execution; the second is projected to be complete by the end of 2027.
  • New clean room. A 15,000-square-foot class 1K clean room has been built for the new high-volume line and is planned to double in size in 2027. Hyperscale customers increasingly require class 1K environments for optical assembly.
  • Investment and timing. Total investment in the new line is expected to exceed $45 million. Equipment is being delivered and installed through approximately April 2027, with the line targeted to be manufacturing-ready by mid-2027.
  • Supply chain. Industry-wide tightening of wafer and component supply in the second half of 2026 affected the Company's output. The Company has secured approximately tenfold wafer capacity, committed early on equipment, and is installing its new engine assembly line, targeted to be manufacturing-ready by mid-2027.

Customer and Product Update

  • Four customers have placed orders of more than $1 million each; three new customers are in the pipeline for four additional projects.
  • Lumilens: development continues on multiple products. The first products are in the assembly development phase and on schedule for verification this year.
  • POET Blazar™ hybrid laser: sampling expected to begin in the first half of 2027, with production targeted for early 2028.
  • 1.6T optical engines are scheduled to ramp to production in 2027.

Non-IFRS Financial Summary

Net loss for the quarter was $7,560,556, or $(0.04) per share, compared with $11,338,060, or $(0.07) per share, in the second quarter of 2026 and $9,369,714, or $(0.11) per share, in the third quarter of 2025. The quarter's results include non-cash items of $5.7 million of stock-based compensation, $1.1 million of depreciation and amortization, a $6.4 million gain on the revaluation of the derivative warrant liability and $122,000 of non-cash finance costs. The warrant revaluation produced a $5.5 million gain in the second quarter of 2026 and a $2.4 million loss in the third quarter of 2025. Cash used in operations was $12.7 million, compared with $12.2 million in the second quarter of 2026. Research and development expense was $5.4 million, compared with $5.8 million in the second quarter, with quarter-to-quarter changes reflecting the timing of development programs and production readiness activities. Cash and short-term investments were $774.3 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $796.3 million at June 30, 2026.

Outlook

The Company expects to ship its current backlog of optical engines, including the 30,000 engines originally targeted for 2026, by the second quarter of 2027, in line with customer delivery schedules and as its new wafer and engine assembly capacity comes online. Fourth-quarter shipments are expected to exceed the Company's total shipments for the first three quarters of 2026, with production running well ahead of shipments to build engines against customer orders for delivery in the first half of 2027. The new engine assembly line is targeted to be manufacturing-ready by mid-2027, Blazar sampling is expected to begin in the first half of 2027, and seven of the Company's nine active customers are expected to enter production with POET engines in 2027. Production timing for each program depends on customers completing qualification of POET engines within their own modules, which can require design changes to the customer's module or, at times, to the Company's engine; longer industry lead times can extend these design cycles. The Company is aligning its production plans with its customers' delivery schedules. The Company will report its fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results on March 1, 2027 and will host its first quarterly conference call that day. The Company will report progress on each program as milestones are reached.

About POET Technologies Inc.

POET is a design and development company offering high-speed optical engines, light source products and custom optical modules to the artificial intelligence systems market and to hyperscale data centers. POET's photonic integration solutions are based on the POET Optical Interposer™, a novel, patented platform that allows the seamless integration of electronic and photonic devices into a single chip using advanced wafer-level semiconductor manufacturing techniques. POET's Optical Interposer-based products are lower cost, consume less power than comparable products, are smaller in size and are readily scalable to high production volumes. In addition to providing high-speed (800G, 1.6T and above) optical engines and optical modules for AI clusters and hyperscale data centers, POET has designed and produced novel light source products for chip-to-chip data communication within and between AI servers, the next frontier for solving bandwidth and latency problems in AI systems. POET's Optical Interposer platform also solves device integration challenges across a broad range of communication, computing and sensing applications. POET is headquartered in Toronto, Canada, with operations in Singapore, Penang, Malaysia and Shenzhen, China. More information about POET is available on its website at www.poet-technologies.com.
