[p1] Merger now expected to close by the end of the first quarter of 2027
[p2] BEVERLY, Mass., and PLAINVIEW, N.Y., November 2, 2026 – Axcelis Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: ACLS) (“Axcelis”) and Veeco Instruments Inc. (NASDAQ: VECO) (“Veeco”) today provided an update on the expected timing of the previously disclosed all-stock merger of Axcelis and Veeco. The companies now expect the transaction to close by the end of the first quarter of 2027, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of all closing conditions. There can be no assurances that the closing will occur on this timeline.
[p3] The completion of the transactions contemplated by the merger agreement is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of customary closing conditions, including the pending regulatory approval from the State Administration for Market Regulation of the People’s Republic of China (“SAMR”). The parties continue to work constructively with SAMR to obtain clearance.
[p4] In addition, due to the pending expiry of the current clearance under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended (the “HSR Act”), the parties plan to promptly make a new submission under the HSR Act. The closing is also conditioned upon the expiration or early termination of the HSR Act waiting period for this new submission.
[p5] Under the terms of the merger agreement, each share of Veeco common stock will be converted into the 0.3575 Axcelis shares at closing. Together, the combined company is expected to be well positioned as a semiconductor equipment leader serving complementary, diversified and expanding end markets.
[p6] Axcelis (Nasdaq: ACLS), headquartered in Beverly, Mass., has been providing innovative, high-productivity solutions for the semiconductor industry for over 45 years. Axcelis is dedicated to developing enabling process applications through the design, manufacture and complete life cycle support of ion implantation systems, one of the most critical and enabling steps in the IC manufacturing process. Learn more about Axcelis at www.axcelis.com.
[p7] Veeco (NASDAQ: VECO) is an innovative manufacturer of semiconductor process equipment. Our laser annealing, ion beam, metal organic chemical vapor deposition (MOCVD), single wafer etch & clean and lithography technologies play an integral role in the fabrication and packaging of advanced semiconductor devices. With equipment designed to optimize performance, yield and cost of ownership, Veeco holds leading technology positions in the markets we serve. To learn more about Veeco's systems and service offerings, visit www.veeco.com.
[p8] Additional Information About the Potential Transaction and Where to Find It
[p9] In connection with the proposed Merger between Axcelis and Veeco pursuant to the Merger Agreement, dated as of September 30, 2025, by and between Axcelis, Veeco and Victory Merger Sub, Inc., on December 8, 2025, Axcelis filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) a registration statement on Form S-4 (File No. 333-292008), as amended on December 29, 2025 (the “Registration Statement”).
